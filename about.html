About

European football,
explained with data

The Coefficient breaks down the game the way a coach and an analyst see it: formations, pressing, build-up patterns, xG and the numbers behind every result.

It's made by a youth soccer coach in Houston who loves the European game. The goal is simple: help players, parents, coaches and young fans learn to see the game differently.
